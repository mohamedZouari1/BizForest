How it works

From Satellite to Decision

The entire pipeline runs from raw satellite imagery to an interactive map your team can act on.

1
GEE Analysis
Sentinel-2, Dynamic World, ESA WorldCover, and Hansen Forest Change fused with a 3-source consensus algorithm.
2
Random Forest
250-tree Random Forest with 34 spectral & terrain features classifies every pixel into 4 land-cover classes.
3
Priority Score
Multi-criteria evaluation: thermal stress (35%) · urban density (30%) · tree distance (20%) · bareness (15%).
4
Map & App
GeoJSON polygons exported and rendered on this web platform and the BizForest Flutter mobile app.
